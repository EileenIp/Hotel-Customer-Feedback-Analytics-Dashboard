# 🏨 Hotel Customer Feedback Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an **interactive customer feedback dashboard** designed to analyse **hotel guest satisfaction, Net Promoter Score (NPS), and service quality** across multiple touchpoints. The dashboard helps hospitality teams understand **what drives guest satisfaction**, identify improvement areas, and track customer sentiment over time.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Measure overall guest satisfaction and loyalty
- Monitor Net Promoter Score (NPS) and customer sentiment
- Identify strengths and weaknesses across hotel services
- Understand guest demographics and visit purpose
- Improve service quality and customer experience (CX)

---

---

## 🧠 Key Insights
- **Staff service** receives the highest volume of feedback, highlighting its importance in guest experience.
- **Rooms and facilities** are key drivers of satisfaction and dissatisfaction.
- Business travellers represent the largest guest segment, followed by vacation travellers.
- Male guests contribute a slightly higher share of feedback compared to female guests.
- Hotel booking platforms and organisations are the most common sources of information.
- NPS indicates moderate customer loyalty with room for improvement.

## 🖥️ Dashboard Features
- Overall satisfaction rating and star-based visualisation
- Net Promoter Score (NPS) gauge and category breakdown
- Feedback volume by service area:
  - Staff
  - Room
  - Facility
  - Restaurant
- Purpose of visit analysis
- Guest demographics by gender
- Source of information analysis and trends over time
- Interactive filters:
  - Service category
  - Gender
  - Date range
